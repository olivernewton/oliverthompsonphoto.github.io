Photo & Film

Storytelling Through Photo & Film

The best wedding photographs and films are not forced. They are felt. I approach weddings with a documentary mindset — quietly observing, creatively capturing, and preserving moments exactly as they happen.

The nervous excitement before the ceremony. The movement and emotion during the vows. The laughter during dinner. The moments you may not even realise are happening until years later.

Whether through photography or film, my goal is never to interrupt the day. It is to preserve it honestly and beautifully.

My Approach

Less posing. More presence.

I am not there to turn your wedding into a production. I am there to tell the story of your day as naturally as possible. That means less posing, less pressure, and more room for genuine moments to happen.

I focus on emotion, movement, light, sound, and connection — documenting the atmosphere of the day in a way that feels effortless and true to you.

When direction is needed, I keep it simple and natural. Nothing stiff or overly staged. Just enough guidance to help you feel comfortable, connected, and fully present with each other.

The result is a collection of photographs and films that feel timeless, emotional, and real.

Creative Philosophy

Capturing The Moments You'll Want To Relive

Some of the most meaningful moments happen quietly. A glance across the room. A parent fixing a tie. Friends laughing during speeches. The sound of your vows.

These are the moments that become memories. I look beyond just how the wedding looked — focusing on how it felt to be there.

Through both photography and videography, I aim to preserve the emotion, energy, and atmosphere that made your day uniquely yours.

Experience

A Calm Presence On Your Wedding Day

Weddings move quickly, and the people documenting your day should make the experience feel effortless — not stressful. I work calmly and intentionally throughout the day, blending into the environment while capturing everything with creativity and care.

Whether photographing moments as they unfold or filming the atmosphere and emotion of the day, my focus is always on helping you stay present. You should be experiencing your wedding — not performing for the camera.

Wedding Films

Films With Emotion & Intention

Wedding films should feel personal, immersive, and honest. I create cinematic films that focus on real emotion and natural moments rather than overly staged scenes.

Through movement, audio, atmosphere, and storytelling, your film becomes more than a highlight reel — it becomes a way to relive the feeling of the day.

Every wedding is different, and every story deserves to be told in a way that feels authentic to the people living it.
